Husband, podcaster, writer, blogger, and community organizer Belghast leaves behind a powerful legacy.
<!--more-->

I really don't know what to say. Life dealt Belghast twin blows; his wife passed away suddenly a year ago, and then followed that up with a diagnosis for cancer.

And through all of it, Belghast wrote about these most terrible things, and did so with such understanding and depth that I felt his strength and compassion was a bottomless well. That I only hoped I could face that kind of hardship with such grace.

> [  Post by @SitaDulip@gamepad.club View on Mastodon ](https://gamepad.club/@SitaDulip/116856497814220097)

There are people in this world that are steady, dependable, an island in a stormy ocean. Someone who just seemed to have enough love and compassion for people they have never met; always a kind word, always looking for something more he could do, some better way he could help. He gave so much.

It'd been clear from his recent posts that chemotherapy was not going well; the lows were lower, the highs not high enough. Still, he wrote. He was so strong.

The first post I could find on his Tales of the Aggronaut blog ended with the words "[Life is Good](https://aggronaut.com/2009/04/27/holidays-suck-noblegarden-edition/)".

When Belghast was with us, life was good, because he was in it.
