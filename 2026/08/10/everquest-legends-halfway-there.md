It's been a pretty eventful day in EQL; I feel like I'm beginning to see the real game.
<!--more-->

I love being in an active guild in EverQuest Legends. Not for grouping, so much, since grouping isn't a thing that necessarily has to happen, but just to see the notices flash across the screen: so-and-so has hit level 30; so-and-so has entered the Plane of Hate; seeing the loot linked. It gives me a real incentive to keep logging in and making my own way through the game.

But I like sightseeing.

And not just the sights, but the memories. Remember when everyone would hit 30 and immediately start working on the class-specific armor? People would be in weird mismatched gear, and then suddenly, bards would be whizzing by in deep blue armor, clerics would be shining in gold, shadow knights would be resplendent in their black and crimson darkforge, druids in their green leather. It felt a little like graduating into the real game.

There's absolutely no reason to do those things in EverQuest Legends. The revamped Befallen and Crushbone will give you gear of equivalent quality much earlier, and that armor will take you through the dragons and into the planes. There is really no reason at all to spend the time, plat and especially the *motes* to do these quests and to power up the armor enough to make them comparable to the stuff farmed when your character was just starting out.

Well, there is one reason. If EverQuest Legends is for anyone, it's for people who loved doing this stuff back in the day and wanted to speedrun those memories. I don't quite remember when I hit level 50 on my main character back in the day. I am pretty sure it was some time after Kunark opened. I hadn't been really working on my leveling, and druids really were expected to solo to max level back then. They, like necromancers, weren't really group-friendly.

I (of course) did all these quests for my druid. And also every single one of my alts. You see why it took me so long to get to max level. When I hit 60, I did it quad-kiting spirocs in Timorous Deep. I'd learned the hard way that druids had jobs in raids, not so much in groups, by then.

But. I played a rogue in EQL beta; the class quest for rogues was the Burning Rapier quest, and so I did it.

For warriors, my primary class in EQL live, the class quest is for Crafted Armor. Centaurs in South Karana give the quests, which each requires a drop from some dungeon or other plus some expensive gems.

Money, I had from killing hill giants in the Rathe Mountains (and I had to go back and kill more; I didn't have *enough* money after all). But now I had to camp rare spawns in North Karana, avocets in South Karana, Floating Eyes in Runnyeye, ice goblins in Permafrost, cinder goblins in Sol A, a named in Najena's Lair (I also killed Najena for my first time in EQL Live while there), and some gypsy musicians (who are secretly werewolves) in Castle Mistmoore.

My last time in Mistmoore was during beta, while I was working on the Burning Rapier quest. I needed a drop from a certain mob. I snuck in, got behind her, gave her a good stab, and then tried to fend off her dozen friends who came by to call just at that moment. I killed her and got the drop and was immediately killed in turn by the adds.

This time, though, I couldn't sneak, as rogue is no longer part of my combo. I could invis in, so I did, but some things see invis, and, well, I got jumped a few times.

Rogue is no longer part of my combo, but Enchanter *is*, and I was ready for AE stuns and AE mesmerizing. I'd fight things at *my* pace, one at a time. Coming into the castle proper, I must have drawn near the whole first floor. No problem. I can keep up to 30 things controlled while I take them down.

I couldn't believe it. I felt so *powerful*. This overpull would have wiped a group that didn't have an enchanter back in the day, and even then, the different way mana was used and restored would put a time limit on killing things or potentially dying.

But nope. Not this time. I worked my way through the mobs, keeping them mezzed, until they all were dead. I walked into the ballroom where the gypsy musicians were lounging around in their ears and fur, and I stunned them, mezzed them, killed them one at a time, and the third one had the drop. I left.

I'd have been *begging* for help with this, at level, back in the day. Or rather, as a warrior, I'd be expected to take hours leading a group there, and then being forced to stay for hours after I got my drop just to be polite.

That was the last bit. I went to the centaurs and got kitted out. I got my motes exchanged for better ones and leveled up each piece until it was at least as good as the piece it replaced. That took almost all my motes. But dammit, when people look at my character, they will see she is a *warrior*.

After dinner, Kasul and I went to Lower Guk, my first time there with this character, and we took down all the good nameds, several times. We both dinged 35. I think he hit 36 in there. Both of us got two Short Swords of the Ykesha -- "yaks". I'd been rocking the Blackened Alloy Bastard Sword for so long that I needed to get my dual wield and 1-hand slashing trained up, so these will be perfect.

Just like in Mistmoore, occasionally we'd want to pull one or two but get seven. Stun. AE Mez. Take them down one at a time.

I think this combo (warrior/cleric/enchanter) is really going to work out.
