Crypto, Crypto, what will you do?

November 29, 2022 in Blockchain, NFT, OpenAI

This song tells the story of a pup named Crypto who dreams of becoming wealthy through trading on various cryptocurrency markets.

Song written by text-davinci-003 given the prompt of an article about FTX.

Verse 1

There once was a pup named Crypto Who dreamed of the riches he’d know He heard of the markets and FTX Where fortunes could ebb and flow

Chorus

Crypto, Crypto, what will you do? Crypto, Crypto, you’re feeling so blue You lost all your cash in FTX And now you don’t have a home

Verse 2

Crypto found trading on Ethereum Where fortunes could be made from the start But the markets were wild and so were the swings And Crypto was taken apart

Chorus

Crypto, Crypto, what will you do? Crypto, Crypto, you’re feeling so blue You lost all your cash in FTX And now you don’t have a home

Chorus

Crypto, Crypto, what will you do? Crypto, Crypto, you’re feeling so blue You lost all your cash in FTX And now you don’t have a home

Crypto, Crypto, what will you do? Crypto, Crypto, you’re feeling so blue You lost all your cash in FTX But you still have your dreams and that’s true
