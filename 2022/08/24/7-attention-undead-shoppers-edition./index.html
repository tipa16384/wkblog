Number: 24. Genre: First-person shooter. Title: “24/7”

The newspaper ad said “Wanted. Security Guard for the City Mall. Night Shift. Experience in Loss Control preferred.” When you applied, you didn’t know that when the mall closed for humans… it opened for the undead.

The night time clientele include zombies, vampires, werewolves and even the occasional ghoul. It’s your job to keep them all in check and make sure they don’t cause too much trouble.

24/7 is a first-person shooter with a horror twist. The typical night for the guard includes patrolling the mall, making sure all the undead creatures are behaving themselves and keeping an eye out for any potential trouble. If any of the creatures get out of line, it’s the guard’s job to put them back in their place. Sometimes this can be a difficult task, especially when there are multiple creatures causing problems at once. But as long as the guard remains calm and keeps a level head, they should be able to handle anything that comes their way.

The guard has access to a variety of different weapons to help them keep the undead under control. These include traditional firearms such as pistols and shotguns, as well as more specialized weapons designed specifically for taking down creatures of the night. The guard also has a flashlight to help them see in the dark, and a first aid kit in case they get injured during their shift.
